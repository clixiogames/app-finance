Privacy policy

APP FINANCE · Last updated 19 November 2026

In short

APP FINANCE does not collect, store or share your personal or financial data. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Data you enter

Accounts, holdings, amounts, dates, income and notes are saved only on your device (in the app's private storage on Android, or in your browser's storage on the website). We have no server and no user accounts, so we cannot see, access or recover this data.

On Android, the system may include the app's data in your own Google device backup if you have enabled it. This is controlled by your device settings and your Google account, not by us.

Price lookups

To show current values, the app downloads public prices and exchange rates. These requests contain only the identifier of the instrument (for example a ticker, an ISIN or a coin name) and dates — never your amounts, balances or any personal information. The sources are:

  • Yahoo Finance public data (funds, ETFs, stocks) — on the website these requests pass through our relay, which forwards them without storing anything;
  • CoinGecko (crypto prices);
  • Frankfurter / European Central Bank (exchange rates).

Like any website, these services can see your device's IP address when they answer a request. Their own privacy policies apply.

Backups

When you save a backup file, it is written where you choose (for example your Downloads folder or Google Drive). You are in control of that file.

No tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.

Deleting your data

Use Settings › Delete all my data, uninstall the app, or clear the website's data in your browser.

Not financial advice

APP FINANCE shows and organises information you enter. It does not provide investment advice and never moves money.
